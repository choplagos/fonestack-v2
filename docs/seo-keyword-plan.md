# Fonestack SEO keyword plan

## Research notes

The target audience is phone buyers, repair customers and people selling or trading in a device in Lagos, especially Ikeja and Computer Village. The plan favors specific, locally relevant searches over broad national head terms.

**SERP validation is incomplete.** Google result pages returned JavaScript-only challenge content in this environment. A Bing check for a Computer Village/Ikeja repair seed surfaced broad national phone retailers and marketplaces rather than a stable, clearly localized result set. No reliable search-volume or keyword-difficulty dataset was available. The difficulty labels below are therefore **unverified estimates**, based on query specificity, local intent and the likely competition of broad retail terms—not measured SERP metrics. Recheck live Google mobile results and Google Maps before prioritizing new pages.

Use current, confirmed catalogue data for live product details. Do not add price ranges, stock statements, repair timing, warranty promises or financing terms without owner confirmation.

### Priority guide

- **P1:** Closely matches an existing service or a high-intent content gap; improve the relevant page first.
- **P2:** Good supporting long-tail target; add after core pages and owner facts are settled.
- **P3:** Broad, competitive query; defer until the site has stronger topical depth and current supporting data.

## Proposed blog posts — approval required before drafting

Link to the fairly used iPhone, sell-phone and trade-in pages.
